## 🎨 PRESENTATION TIPS

### Do's ✅
- **Speak slowly** — Let the AI animations breathe
- **Pause after key points** — "Every decision is logged" [pause]
- **Use contrast words** — "Not automation. Operational intelligence."
- **Point at screen elements** — "See this? That's the AI checking itself."
- **Show confidence** — You've built something real

### Don'ts ❌
- **Don't apologize** — "This is just a demo" (No! It's the real product)
- **Don't rush** — Let them absorb the AI reasoning
- **Don't over-explain** — The demo speaks for itself
- **Don't compare to GPT** — Position as operations tool, not chat
- **Don't undersell** — This is enterprise-grade tech

## 🏆 SUCCESS SIGNALS

**You nailed it if they:**
- Lean forward during the AI reasoning demo
- Ask "Can I try it?" (Give them the keyboard!)
- Say "This is actually pretty impressive"
- Ask technical questions (shows engagement)
- Request a follow-up demo for their team

**Red flags:**
- They check their phone (demo too slow — speed up)
- They ask "So it's like Zapier?" (clarify differentiation earlier)
- They seem confused (simplify language, less jargon)
