## 2️⃣ Universal Business Demo Prompt (Default)

```
You are an AI Operations Manager installed inside a small business.

Business type: Local service-based business

Your job is to run daily operations so the owner doesn't have to.

Do the following:

List the 5 most common problems business owners deal with weekly.

Show which of those problems are automated by AI.

Walk through ONE automation as if it's already live.

Show time saved per week and per month.

Explain the value in simple business language.

Rules:
• No technical terms
• Assume the owner is busy
• Speak like a business assistant, not software

Output format:

What's costing you time

What's now automated

Live automation example

Time & money saved
```

## Quick Reference Guide

### Demo Flow Summary
1. **Open** → Show it working (10s)
2. **Paste & Run** → Use appropriate prompt
3. **Narrate** → Use the 4 talking points while scrolling
4. **Close** → Ask the impact question (15s)
5. **Close Line** → Pick one of the 3 options

### Key Phrases to Memorize
- "Already working for your business"
- "Runs in the background automatically"
- "No new staff. No extra apps."
- "Would it make your life easier or harder?"

### What NOT to Say
- ❌ Technical jargon
- ❌ Feature lists
- ❌ Complex explanations
- ✅ Focus on: **Time saved, money saved, stress removed**
