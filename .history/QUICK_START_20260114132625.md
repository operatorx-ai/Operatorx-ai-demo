### Don'ts ❌

### "Can I see more examples?"
"Absolutely. [Run another industry demo] Or want to see YOUR actual data?"

### "This seems too good to be true"
"I get it. That's why we offer a 30-day guarantee. Try it for a month. If it doesn't deliver, full refund."
